Questions

Asked often.

How much space do you need?

Six feet against a wall for the bar cart. Add three feet behind us to work in.

Do you need power and water?

Nice to have, not required. Two standard outlets on separate circuits is ideal; otherwise we run off battery and bring our own water. We bring enough ice for your event.

How far in advance should we book?

3-4 weeks is comfortable. Weekends in spring and fall go earlier than that, and we do take last-minute if the calendar allows.

Can guests pay, or is it hosted?

Either. Hosted is the norm for private events; for markets and open houses we can run a card reader and take orders directly.

Do you still have a café we can visit?

Not a storefront — the bar goes where the people are now. Tastings happen at our prep space, and you'll find us at markets and pop-ups most months.
