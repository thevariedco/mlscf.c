We started in the neighborhood, and kept the neighbors with us.

Miles Café was born from The Miles Coffee Bar, a neighborhood café with warm vibes, and a regular crowd. The bar is mobile now — we come to the crowd.

Our story

Ten years, one true obsession.

The first decade happened behind a counter — as a barista, a manager, and then a partner; the person who made every detail was perfect before anyone else showed up. That's where the standards came from. Not from a book.

A neighborhood café where the first scents to decorate the air were fresh grounds and bacon. Where the neighborhood held meetings, and where people came back not for the coffee but for the culture we had cultivated.

The thing we kept hearing was: can you bring this to us? A wedding, an office, a Saturday market. So we built a bar that travels — the same espresso, the same syrups cooked by hand, the same greeting — and stopped waiting for people to walk past.

A café taught us hospitality on a fixed floor plan. Going mobile returns the favor in your own space.
